A SMALL GUIDE

Good to know.

Here's what you can actually do in this concept experience.

Can I book a real stay through this website? +

No. Selara is a fictional resort concept made to demonstrate a high-quality hospitality website. There are no real rooms, inventory, confirmations or payments.

What can I interact with here? +

Filter the stays, save your favorites in this browser, explore experience descriptions, choose dates and guests, and export or print your sample trip summary.

Will the website remember my preferences? +

Saved favorites and trip details are kept in local storage in this browser only. You can clear them below at any time. Private/incognito browsing or clearing site data may erase them.

How are prices calculated? +

We multiply the fictional nightly room price by the number of selected nights. No actual live rates, taxes or availability are involved.
